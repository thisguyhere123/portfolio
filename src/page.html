About

Most of what I build reads content from the open web, so I design every system on the assumption that its input is hostile. My multi-agent AI research pipeline treats every scraped page as untrusted data: hidden text is stripped and each document is fenced off so it can't inject instructions into the model. Its report can't print a number or claim it can't trace to a source.

Scrape Anything, my web-scraping service, fetches any URL a user hands it, so it blocks private networks and cloud metadata addresses, re-checks every redirect, and signs its webhooks. Who Owns Your Hospital? is a live public-data site I run on my own hardened Linux server.

I work in Python, TypeScript and C#, and I care about tests, least privilege, and software that does what it says.
